What is an
Oopbuy spreadsheet?

An Oopbuy spreadsheet is a product-link list used to organize shopping research. Useful entries include a source link, category, variant, measurements, price notes and any available quality-control photos. A spreadsheet does not guarantee stock, authenticity or seller reliability. This page offers a practical guide and CAFANS category links; it does not provide a downloadable spreadsheet.

A practical browsing routine

From discovery to a useful shortlist.

For an Oopbuy shopping guide to be useful, it should help you make comparisons you can verify.

01

Choose one category

Begin with an Oopbuy shoes spreadsheet or an Oopbuy clothing spreadsheet topic. Keep footwear, layers and accessories in separate groups so their details are easier to compare.

02

Capture the exact variant

Save the source URL, color, size and measurements. Oopbuy product links are only a starting point: a different variant can have different dimensions, photos or availability.

03

Recheck before paying

Review current listing details, applicable policies and delivery costs. When researching Oopbuy QC photos, distinguish seller images from photos of the actual item being inspected.

Build a better comparison sheet

The details worth recording.

A small set of consistent fields makes a shortlist easier to revisit and update.

Field What to record Why it helps
Source & category Original URL, product name and category. Return to the source and avoid confusing similar items.
Variant & fit Color, selected size, measurements and units. Compare the actual variant with something you already own.
Material & construction Listed fabric, composition, closure and care information. Keep verified listing details separate from assumptions.
Photo evidence Image source, visible details and any measurement photos. Understand what a photo shows and what remains uncertain.
Estimated total Item price, delivery, applicable fees and import charges. Compare the likely overall cost rather than the headline price.
Checked date When the price, link and availability were last reviewed. Recognize entries that may need another check.

What is an Oopbuy spreadsheet?

An Oopbuy spreadsheet is a product-link list used to organize shopping research. Useful entries include a source link, category, variant, measurements, price notes and any available quality-control photos. A spreadsheet does not guarantee stock, authenticity or seller reliability.

Does this page provide a downloadable spreadsheet?

No. This page provides a browsing guide and links to CAFANS categories. You can organize your own shortlist in a spreadsheet using the comparison fields described here.

How should I compare clothing sizes?

Compare the listing’s garment measurements with a similar item you already own. Check the units, measurement method and size chart for the exact product and variant.

What can QC photos tell me?

Quality-control photos can help you inspect visible details such as color, stitching, labels and measurements. They cannot establish authenticity, long-term durability or a guaranteed fit.

Is this an official Oopbuy or brand website?

No. This is an independent informational page linking to CAFANS. It does not claim affiliation with Oopbuy, Nike, Louis Vuitton, Gucci or Hermès.

How do I estimate the total shopping cost?

Consider the item price, domestic delivery, any applicable service fees, international shipping and applicable import charges. Verify current amounts and conditions with the relevant providers before paying.
